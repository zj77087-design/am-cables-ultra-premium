INFORMED PRODUCT SELECTION

Product Details Matter

Electrical wires and cables can differ in conductor material, cross-sectional area, insulation, construction and intended use. A product name alone may not provide all the information needed to determine whether it is suitable for a particular installation.

Before ordering, ask for the specifications relevant to your application and verify that the product meets your project's requirements and applicable electrical standards.

A.M. Cables customers can contact the team to discuss a product and confirm the information available for that item.

BEFORE CONFIRMATION

What Should You Verify?

  • Exact product name and identification
  • Conductor material and size
  • Insulation type and rating
  • Required length and order quantity
  • Suitability for the intended installation
  • Availability and agreed order details
IMPORTANT DETAILS

What to Ask About a Cable

These are useful questions to discuss with the supplier or a qualified electrical professional.

Conductor Material

Confirm whether the conductor is copper or another material, and request clarification about the exact product description.

Size & Gauge

Ask for the exact conductor cross-sectional area or gauge. Do not rely on a product nickname alone.

Insulation

Confirm the insulation material, voltage rating and installation suitability for the intended use.

Length & Quantity

Confirm the supplied length, ordering unit, required quantity and any minimum order conditions before agreeing to purchase.

Technical Documentation

Where applicable, ask whether a technical datasheet, test report or relevant conformity documentation is available.

Application Suitability

Check that the selected cable meets the load, environment, installation method and electrical code requirements.
