Quotation FAQs

Does submitting the form confirm my order?

No. It prepares a WhatsApp message. Your order is not confirmed until the details are discussed and agreed directly.

What if I don't know the product size?

Select the closest product name if known and explain your requirements in the message. Ask the team to confirm the correct specifications for your intended application.

Can I ask about bulk quantities?

Yes. Include your required quantity, unit and delivery area so the request can be discussed.

How do I send the request?

Submit the form, review the prepared message in WhatsApp, then press Send to contact A.M. Cables.
