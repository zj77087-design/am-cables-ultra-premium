COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

A few helpful answers before you contact our team.

How can I find a particular product?

Visit the Products page and use the search field or material filter to find a listed item.

Can I ask about a product's gauge and length?

Yes. Contact our team with the product name and ask to confirm the available size, length and other specifications.

How do I request a quotation?

Open the Order page, provide your contact details and share the product and quantity requirements you know.

Can I discuss a bulk or project requirement?

You can submit a project or bulk inquiry. Include the product names and estimated quantities so the team can review your request.

How do I confirm whether a cable suits my installation?

Confirm the technical specifications and consult a qualified electrician to check suitability for your installation and applicable electrical standards.
